Privacy Policy

Golf Tracker · Effective October 7, 2026

Golf Tracker does not collect your data. What you enter stays on your device, and, if you choose, in your own iCloud. We never see it.

What the app stores

To work, the app keeps the information you enter: rounds (date, course name, play type, tees, course rating and slope, scores, hole-by-hole scores and notes) and the courses you save. Your handicap index and statistics are calculated on your device from that information.

Where it is stored

  • On your device. The data lives in the app's storage on your iPhone. It is included in your normal iPhone backups, like other app data.
  • In your iCloud (optional). If you are signed in to iCloud, the app also saves a copy to your private iCloud storage so your data can sync across your own devices and be restored on a new one. This copy is held by Apple under your Apple ID, is governed by Apple's privacy policy, and is not accessible to us. You can turn this off in iOS Settings by switching off iCloud for the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not have accounts, logins or a server, and the app does not send your data to us.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking or any third-party SDKs.
  • We do not sell, share or rent your information, because we do not have it.
  • We do not ask for your location, contacts, photos or any other device permissions.

Exporting your data

The app can export your rounds as a CSV file. This only happens when you ask for it, and the file goes wherever you choose to send it. Once it leaves the app, it is outside our control.

Deleting your data

  • Delete individual rounds and courses from inside the app, or use Rounds → Delete all data to remove everything, including the copy synced to your iCloud.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device, but does not by itself remove a copy saved in your iCloud. To remove both, use Delete all data before you delete the app.

Notes you write

The notes field is free text. Anything you type there, including anything personal, is handled exactly like the rest of your data described above.

Children

Golf Tracker is not directed to children, and because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect any from anyone.

Handicap calculations

The handicap index shown is an estimate calculated from the scores you enter, using the World Handicap System method. It is not an official USGA Handicap Index or an authorised handicap from a golf association.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If a future version of the app collects any data, this policy will be updated before it does.
