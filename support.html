Common questions

How is my handicap index calculated?

For each round, the app works out a differential: (113 ÷ slope) × (your gross score − course rating). The index is the average of the best 8 differentials from your most recent 20 rounds, multiplied by 0.96. With fewer than 20 rounds it uses fewer differentials, following the World Handicap System table. You need at least 3 rounds before an index appears. On the Home screen, the differentials that count are marked with a dot.

How do I scan a scorecard?

Open Rounds → My courses → Add course, then tap Scan a scorecard. Take a photo of the card laid flat, in good light, with the whole table in view, or choose a photo you already have. The app reads it on your iPhone and fills in the course name, each tee's rating and slope, the hole pars and the stroke index it can find. Nothing is saved until you check the numbers against your card and tap Save course. If part of the card can't be read, the app tells you what's missing so you can type it in. The photo isn't stored or sent anywhere.

How do 9-hole rounds work?

When you log a round, choose Front 9 or Back 9 and enter that nine's own rating and slope from the scorecard. The app works out a 9-hole score differential, adds the differential you would be expected to shoot over the other nine (based on the index you had going into the round), and uses the result as an 18-hole differential. The expected value is an approximation, because the official table isn't published, so a 9-hole round is an estimate on top of an estimate. 9-hole rounds are kept out of your average and best scores, and they only count toward your index once you already have one.

What is net double bogey, and does the app apply it?

For handicap purposes, no single hole counts for more than par + 2 + the strokes you receive on it (par + 5 before you have an index). When you score a full round hole by hole, and the course's hole pars and stroke index are saved under My courses, the app applies this for you and uses the adjusted total. You can override it by typing your own adjusted gross. 9-hole rounds are not adjusted automatically.

How do I play a round hole by hole?

On the Home screen, tap Play hole by hole, choose your course and tees, and start. For each hole, tap Birdie, Par, Bogey or Double, or use the plus and minus buttons, then move to the next hole. Your round is saved as you go, so you can close the app and come back to it from the Home screen. When you reach the end, review the scores, then tap Save round. To save the pars for a course, set or confirm the par on each hole as you play, or enter them under My courses.

What does "To lower your index" mean?

It shows the highest score at your most recent course and tees that would bring your estimated index down, and which of your last 20 rounds is about to drop out. It uses the same calculation as your index, so it follows the method you choose in Settings.

Can I import my rounds?

Yes. Open the Rounds tab, scroll to Data, and tap Import CSV. The file needs a header row with at least date, course, rating, slope and gross columns. The easiest way to see the format is to export your rounds first. Rounds already in the app are skipped, and any row with a problem is listed by line number so you can fix it.

Is this my official handicap?

No. It is an estimate calculated from the scores you enter. It is not an official USGA Handicap Index or an authorised handicap from a golf association, so it may differ from the number your club or association reports.

Where do I find the course rating and slope?

They are printed on the scorecard, usually listed for each set of tees. Many courses also publish them on their website. Once you save a course, the app remembers its tees, rating and slope so you only enter them once.

Does my data sync between devices?

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app backs up your rounds and courses to your private iCloud storage and syncs them between your own devices. Open the Rounds tab and look under Data to see whether iCloud sync is on. If it says it is off, sign in to iCloud in iOS Settings. Changes can take a few moments to appear on another device.

How do I get my data out?

Open the Rounds tab, scroll to Data, and tap Export CSV. Choose where to send the file, such as Save to Files, AirDrop or Mail. It opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a single round or course from its edit screen. To remove everything, including the copy in your iCloud, open Rounds and tap Delete all data. Do this before deleting the app if you want the iCloud copy gone too.

I got a new iPhone. How do I move my rounds?

Sign in to the same iCloud account and install Golf Tracker. Your rounds and courses will appear after a short sync. A restored iPhone backup brings them over as well.
